Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before creating your Familytoon.

Is Familytoon free?

Familytoon is planned as a one-time paid download with no recurring subscription.

Can I download my finished artwork?

Yes. Familytoon lets you save your finished family cartoon as a high-quality PNG image.

Does the PNG have a transparent background?

Yes, when you choose the transparent-background export option available in the app.

Can I print my Familytoon artwork?

Yes. You can use your downloaded PNG for personal projects such as shirts, mugs, cards, posters, invitations, and gifts.

Can I create large families?

Yes. Familytoon is designed to let you combine multiple family members and pets.

Does Familytoon use artificial intelligence?

No. You create your artwork by selecting and combining the app’s original character parts and customization options.

Do I need an account?

No account is planned for the basic character creation and image download experience.

How do I contact support?

Email Support at FamilytoonApp.com or visit the Support page.
