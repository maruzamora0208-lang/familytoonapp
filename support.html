WE’RE HERE TO HELP

Familytoon Support

Questions, technical issues, feedback, and feature suggestions are welcome.

Contact Support

Email us at:

support@familytoonapp.com

We usually respond within 24–48 hours.

Include These Details

  • Your iPhone or iPad model
  • Your iOS or iPadOS version
  • Your Familytoon app version
  • A clear description of the problem
  • A screenshot, when helpful

Before Contacting Support

  • Close and reopen the Familytoon app.
  • Make sure your device has enough available storage.
  • Check that Familytoon has Photos permission when saving images.
  • Restart your device and try again.

App Store Purchases

Apple manages App Store purchases, payment methods, receipts, and refunds. Purchase-related requests may need to be handled directly through Apple.
